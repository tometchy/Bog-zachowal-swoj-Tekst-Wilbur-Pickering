Hoskier’s collations prove that there certainly is no **K^x^** in the Apocalypse. We are indebted to the *Institut für Neutestamentliche Text- forschung* for their *Text und Textwert* series. A careful look at their collations indicates that there probably is no **K^x^**, anywhere. Take, for example, the *TuT* volumes on John’s Gospel, chapters 1-10. They examined a total of 1,763 MSS (for 153 variant sets) and included the results in the two volumes. Pages 54 - 90 (volume 1) contain “Groupings according to degrees of agreement” “agreeing more often with each other than with the majority text”. Only one group symbol is used, precisely **K^r^**—the first representative of the family, MS 18, heads a group of about 120 MSS, but all subsequent representatives have only a **K^r^**. Of the 120, the last six show 98%, all the rest are 99% (74) or 100% (40). I would say that Family 35 in the Gospels has over 250 representatives; the ranking here is based on only 153 variant sets (but see what happens below).
