**Postscript**

Family 35 readings are attested by early witnesses, but without pattern, and therefore without dependency. But there are many hundreds of such readings. So how did the **f^35^** archetype come by all those early readings? Did its creator travel around and collect a few readings from Aleph, a few from B, a few from P^45,66,75^, a few from W and D, etc.? Is not such a suggestion patently ridiculous? The only reasonable conclusion is that the **f^35^** text is ancient (also independent).

I claim to have demonstrated the superiority of Family 35 based on size (number of representatives), independence, age, geographical distribution, profile (empirically determined), care (see above) and range (all 27 books). I challenge any and all to do the same for any other line of transmission!
