## *Implications*

I begin with the last column in the chart, ‘Exemplar’. Except for 18, 928, 1864 and 2723 that are themselves perfect, most of the others have a different rating. All singular readings should be discounted (including homoioteleuton and inattention); if not introduced by the copyist it was done by the ‘father’ or ‘grandfather’—an ancestor was free of all ‘singulars’, so they contribute nothing to the history of the transmission, are not relevant to the tracing of that transmission.
