# **But is the archetypal text of f^35^** **the Autograph?**

As they used to say in another world, long departed, “That’s the $64 question”. In Part III I present further objective evidence in support of the claim that the text of **f^35^** is ancient and independent of all other lines of transmission. If **f^35^** is independent of all other lines of transmission then it must hark back to the Autographs. What other reasonable explanation is there? If anyone has a different explanation that accounts for the evidence better than (or as well as) mine does, I would like to see it.[^1]

[^1]: Should anyone wish to claim that **f^35^** is a recension, I request (and insist) that he specify who did it, when and where, and furnish evidence in support of the claim. Without evidence any such claim is frivolous and irresponsible—Hort’s claim that his ‘Syrian’ text was the result of a ‘Lucianic’ recension is a classic example (Burgon protested at the complete lack of evidence, at the time, and no one has come up with any since).
